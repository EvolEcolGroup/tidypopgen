# Distruct colours

Colours in the palette used by distruct

## Usage

``` r
distruct_colours
```

## Format

A vector of 56 hex colours

## Details

The 56 distinct colours of the palette of distruct (which repeats 4 of
its 60 colours), in their original order.
[`scale_fill_distruct()`](https://evolecolgroup.github.io/tidypopgen/reference/scale_fill_distruct.md)
recycles them when there are more groups.
