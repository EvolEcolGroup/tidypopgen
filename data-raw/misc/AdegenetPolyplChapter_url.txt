The example script of the tutorial on polyploid population genomics by
Magdalena Bohutínská and Filip Kolář (Part B, genetic structure with
adegenet), whose data were used to make inst/extdata/ploidy/
arenosa_ploidy_example.vcf.gz (see data-raw/create_ploidy_vcf.R):

https://github.com/mbohutinska/PolyplChapter/blob/main/PartB/AdegenetPolyplChapter.R

The tutorial accompanies Bohutínská M. et al. (2023) Population genomic
analysis of diploid-autopolyploid species. In: Polyploidy, Methods in
Molecular Biology 2545: 297-324. doi:10.1007/978-1-0716-2561-3_16.

The script is not copied here, as its repository has no licence.
